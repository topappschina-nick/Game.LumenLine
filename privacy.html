Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

This policy describes how Lumen Line (the “game”), published by YIWEN DENG (an individual developer, “we”), handles information. It applies to the iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android versions of the game and to this website.

Summary

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. Lumen Line has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and you can play it without an internet connection. The only online activity belongs to the in-app purchase on iPhone and iPad: the game asks the Apple App Store for the price and whether you own the full version, and buying or restoring it goes through the App Store (see In-app purchase).

What the game stores on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps these files in its private storage on your device:

  • your progress: the levels you have completed with your moves and stars, the level you are on, and which light parts the game has already shown you;
  • your daily puzzle results and your streak;
  • your settings: sound, music, effects and language;
  • whether you own the full version.

These files contain only game data, nothing that identifies you, and the game does not send them anywhere. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

On iOS, your device’s own backup (iCloud or a computer backup) may include these files, according to your device settings. The Android version opts out of Android’s cloud backup of app data; a direct phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new Android phone may still copy them, depending on the phone. Backups and transfers are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms, and we have no access to them.

The daily puzzles come with the game: it picks today’s puzzle from your device’s date, without going online.

Copying your result

After a daily puzzle you can tap Copy Result. The game then puts a short text on your device’s clipboard (the date, the puzzle’s name, your stars, moves and streak) so that you can paste it wherever you like. The game writes to the clipboard only when you tap that button, and never reads from it. Where you paste the text is up to you; it is not sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage statistics or crash reports, and we do not sell or share personal data. The game contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting. The only part of the game that uses the internet is the purchase on iPhone and iPad, which uses Apple’s StoreKit, part of iOS (see below).

Permissions

The iOS version does not ask for any permission. The Android version requests only vibration, used for light haptic feedback during play; it does not request internet access. The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

In-app purchase

Lumen Line is free to download. Levels 1-1 to 1-5 and the daily puzzle are free to play. A single one-time in-app purchase (“Full Version”) unlocks the other 75 levels: the rest of Chapter 1 and Chapters 2 to 4. On iPhone and iPad, the purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details, your name or your Apple ID.

To show the price and to know whether you own the purchase, the game asks the App Store on your device: when the game starts, when you return to it, when you buy and when you tap “Restore Purchases”. The App Store answers from your Apple ID, which needs an internet connection; the game talks only to the App Store, never to a server of ours. If the App Store cannot be reached, the game keeps its last answer (saved on your device), so you can keep playing offline. We do not run a server and keep no record of purchases. Apple’s privacy policy applies to your payment and purchase data.

The Android version does not offer the purchase yet and does not connect to the internet. If a later Android version sells the full version through Google Play, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Children

Lumen Line is a gentle puzzle game suitable for all ages, including children. It has no violence, no frightening content, no advertising, no analytics, no chat or contact with other players, and no links out of the game, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The one purchase is made through the App Store, where Apple’s parental controls (such as Ask to Buy) apply. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Third parties

You get the game through the Apple App Store (or Google Play, for Android), which may collect information about your download, your purchases and your use of their store under their own policies (Apple, Google); this includes the purchase processing described above. We do not receive personal information from them about you. This website is static: it uses no cookies, analytics, tracking scripts or external fonts. The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which, like any web host, may process technical request data (such as your IP address) in its server logs under its own policy; we do not have access to that data in a way that identifies you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct, delete or export. Your saves are on your device and under your control. Data about your purchases is held by Apple (or Google); to exercise your rights over it, use their privacy tools and contacts (linked above). Depending on where you live, you have rights over personal data, including under the EU and UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority), California’s CCPA/CPRA (to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information), and Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI). To make any request or ask a question, email us at the address below; if we hold nothing about you, we will tell you so.

We process no personal data of players, so there is no legal basis to state under the GDPR; if you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and delete them when no longer needed. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not transfer it internationally.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game handles data differently (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.

Lumen Line

A puzzle game of light and colour: wake the sleeping stars, one beam at a time.

The stars in the night sky have fallen asleep. Turn mirrors, split beams and mix colours to send each star a light of exactly its own colour; when it wakes, it rises into the sky and joins its constellation. 80 hand-made levels in four chapters (First Light, Rainbow Bay, Star Gates and Clock Tower), plus a new puzzle every day in Tonight’s Sky.

For iPhone and iPad. Free to start: levels 1-1 to 1-5 and the daily puzzle are free, and one purchase unlocks the other 75 levels. Plays offline. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

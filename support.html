Support

Need help? Check the answers below, or email us. In the game, Settings is the gear button at the top right of the screen, on the title screen and during a level.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. The same button is also on the Full Version card that opens when you tap a locked star (one with a small lock) on the Star Map. Make sure you are online and signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. The purchase belongs to your Apple ID, not to us. Once the full version is unlocked, Settings says “Full version unlocked. Thank you for your support!”

I paid, but the levels are still locked.

Try Restore Purchases first. If the card says Waiting for approval (for example, with Ask to Buy in Family Sharing), the levels open as soon as the purchase is approved. Refunds are handled by Apple: see support.apple.com.

How is my progress saved?

Automatically, on your device: the levels you have lit up and their stars, your daily puzzle results and your streak. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Your progress stays when you close or update the game, and is removed if you uninstall the game. Your purchase is not part of the save: it belongs to your Apple ID and can always be restored.

Can I move to a new phone?

Your purchase moves with your Apple ID: on the new iPhone or iPad, sign in with the same Apple ID, install the game and tap Restore Purchases. Your progress is saved only on the old device and the game has no online sync. Setting up the new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one usually brings your progress along.

How do I change the language?

The game starts in your device’s language when it can. To change it, open Settings, tap Language and pick one; System follows your device again. There are 17 languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Русский, Türkçe, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, ไทย, हिन्दी and العربية.

I can’t hear anything.

Open Settings and check that Sound and Music are On; tap a switch to change it. Also check your device’s volume and, on iPhone, whether Silent mode is on.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play: all the levels and the daily puzzles are inside the game. Seeing the price, buying the full version and restoring the purchase need a connection to the App Store. Once you own it, the game remembers it on your device, so you can keep playing offline.
